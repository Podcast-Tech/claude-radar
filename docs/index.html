Turn on iPhone alerts
So GoGo can ping you when the app is closed.

Add GoGo to your iPhone

iPhone only allows alerts from apps on the Home Screen. It takes 10 seconds:

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open GoGo from your Home Screen and sign in.

Open this page in Safari first (other browsers can't add it to the Home Screen).
