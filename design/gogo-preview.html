How it works on the phone

  • Landscape on a stand: GoGo on the left, the newest chat card on the right.
  • Swipe up for every chat as cards. Tap one to mark it seen or open it.
  • Chirps are short tones made by the app, so there's nothing to license. Recording mode mutes them.
  • Night puts on a sleeping cap and dims the screen.
  • Notifications, the live connection and Alert check work the same as now.
